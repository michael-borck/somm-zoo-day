Could you have caught it?

An early build of UDL Lens carried 21 UDL “checkpoints”. Sixteen of the codes were wrong — invented by a language model, plausibly worded, correctly numbered, confidently wrong. Four of the eight below are verbatim UDL 3.0. Four are not. No marks are recorded anywhere.

What can your assessments actually show?

A UDL guideline can only be evidenced by an assessment that puts it in play — an exam cannot show you much about collaboration. Choose the assessment types in one of your units and see how much of UDL 3.0 they can reach between them.
